# Visual Plan

## Core Mood

Atmospheric psychology with existential aesthetic, philosophical dreamcore, existential retro texture, urban melancholy, cinematic loneliness, and slow documentary pacing.

Avoid motivational editing, sigma aesthetics, TikTok rhythm, fast punchy cuts, huge black subtitle rectangles, and random stock footage without emotional relation to the narration.

## Montage Rhythm

- Minimum shot duration: 5 seconds.
- Average shot duration: 8-15 seconds.
- Emotional scenes: 15-25 seconds.
- Maximum intended shot duration: 25 seconds.
- Use long observational shots, soft crossfades, slow zooms, and subtle pans.
- Cuts should follow narration pauses and emotional transitions, not a social-media beat.

## Search Direction

INTRO: rain apartment window, phone glow face, lonely room night, night city reflections, tired eyes, empty subway.

BLOCK 1: digital overload, notifications, people in phones, subway loneliness, screen glow, city crowd isolation.

BLOCK 2: late night scrolling, exhausted room, dark kitchen night, endless feed, rain on glass, tired person bed phone.

BLOCK 3: urban melancholy, empty subway, blurred city lights, lonely walking rain, window reflection, modern loneliness.

BLOCK 4: overwhelmed desk, tabs overload, late night monitor, empty stare, tired worker night, psychological exhaustion.

FINAL: fog forest, calm lake, empty road, morning silence, slow clouds, quiet nature.

## Subtitles

Lower-third cinematic subtitles only. Use a soft translucent background with a readability gradient. No giant black cards, no TikTok style, no word-by-word punch captions.
