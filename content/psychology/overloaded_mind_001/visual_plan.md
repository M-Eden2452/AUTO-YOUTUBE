# Visual Plan

## Core Mood

Atmospheric psychology, cinematic loneliness, urban exhaustion, calm intelligence, modern emotional burnout.

Avoid motivational editing, TikTok pacing, sigma aesthetics, fast punchy cuts, oversized subtitles, hard black subtitle bars.

## Montage Rhythm

- Minimum shot duration: 5 seconds.
- Average shot duration: 8-15 seconds.
- Emotional scenes can hold for 20-25 seconds.
- Use breathing rhythm: image changes follow narration pauses and emotional pivots.
- Cuts should feel observed, not performed.

## Scene Language

1. Cold open: night city rain, a single apartment window, slow empty street.
2. Overload definition: screen glow, desk, tabs, hands still above keyboard.
3. Invisible labor: commuter faces, subway reflections, city after work.
4. Guilt: person alone in room, phone beside them, delayed messages.
5. Infographic `diagram_01`: cognitive load vs visible task size.
6. Open loops: browser tabs, notes, calendar, unread messages.
7. Infographic `diagram_02`: lazy vs overloaded distinction.
8. Attention protection: fog, slow train window, shallow focus.
9. Reframing: quiet notebook, closing laptop, rain softening.
10. Infographic `diagram_03`: release pressure -> regain clarity -> one next step.
11. Closing: morning grey light, city still quiet, one person walking slowly.
